FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is the difference between editing and proofreading?

Editing improves the writing itself: structure and pacing (developmental), flow and tone (line), and clarity and consistency (copy). Proofreading is the final polish that catches typos, grammar and punctuation slips once everything else is done.

Which book sizes do you format?

Any size. Tell me your trim size when you message me and I will format to it, for paperback and eBook.

Can you format for platforms other than Amazon KDP?

Yes. My formatting is prepared for Amazon KDP and for other publishing platforms. Mention where you plan to publish and I will prepare your files accordingly.

Will I see every change you make?

Yes. Edits are delivered with tracked changes and comments, so you can accept, reject or discuss each one.

Do you design book covers?

Yes. I design front covers, and full wraps with spine and back cover, for every genre. Browse the portfolio above for examples.

Which genres do you work with?

Fiction, nonfiction, children’s books, Christian and religious books, academic works and more.
